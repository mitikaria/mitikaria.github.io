What sits behind the work

Most communication problems
are not writing problems.
They are structure problems.

My work sits between message, audience and delivery. I have coordinated communications across public-facing events, stakeholder updates, campaign content, sponsor outreach, audience repositioning and community engagement campaign concepts where the message needed to be clear and the process needed to hold.

The common thread is structure: translating scattered information into a clearer message, building the route it needs to travel, and making sure the right people get the right information at the right time.
